About Me

My name is Mohammad Ahshanul Hoq Abir. I was born on 15 March 1995 in Nalua, Satkania, Chattogram, Bangladesh. I began my education at Kaderiya Mainul Uloom Ebtedaye Madrasa. Later, I continued my secondary education at Habibul Uloom Islamia Dakhil Madrasa and completed my higher secondary studies at Bandarban Government College. From an early age, I dreamed of becoming a Computer Engineer. Technology always fascinated me, and I enjoyed learning how digital tools could solve problems and create new opportunities. Alongside my interest in technology, I developed a deep love for nature. Spending time outdoors inspired me to capture the beauty of landscapes, light, and everyday moments through photography. Over time, this passion became an important part of who I am. Today, I continue to grow both as a photography enthusiast and as someone who enjoys building and improving digital experiences. I believe that learning never stops, and every new challenge is an opportunity to become better than yesterday. This website is more than just a portfolio—it's a reflection of my journey, my passion, and my commitment to continuous growth.
